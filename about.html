02OUR APPROACH

Software should
give time back.

Tagging, numbering, sheets, exports and transmittals can absorb hours each week. Our aim is simple: make those repetitive parts of practice more manageable, without asking you to change the tools you already know.

We favour small applications with clear boundaries and interfaces that make sense in a working office.
